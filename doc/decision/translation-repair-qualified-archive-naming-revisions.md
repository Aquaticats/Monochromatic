# Qualified archive naming revisions

Engineering decision under the owner's delegation to make the best preventive changes.
The [archive evidence design][design]
records the measurements and rejected alternatives.
This selects an implementation;
it does not declare the pipeline ready.

## Decision

Use a qualified,
occurrence-scoped naming revision only when both facts are established:

- Independent initial-archive observations corroborate that the complete span is a person,
  group or place reference name.
- Pinned same-path history records an unambiguous replacement whose changed current span
  exactly equals that complete reference-name occurrence.

The conjunction permits the renderer's derived phrase
“records a local English naming choice.”
This is an operational term,
not a statement about editor intent:

- “Records” means verified intrinsic history contains the replacement.
- “Local” means this exact initial-archive occurrence only.
- “English” names the English archive channel,
  not linguistic correctness or officiality.
- “Naming” means corroborated current reference use.
- “Choice” means the recorded current form,
  not deliberation or preference.

Raw history remains neutral fragments and provenance.
The classifier corroborates current use;
existing name policy supplies the defeasible retention consequence.
The predecessor need not have been a name.
Neither an arbitrary historical edit nor a model's use classification alone provides that evidence.
No source-name alias table,
canonical replacement registry or cross-entry glossary is created.

## Why this choice

The naming-specific revision treatment passed the relevant naming and factual controls,
real administrator editing,
the name-only selector comparison in both orders,
and the complete slice repair.
The complete repair retained `Harunome Hanbai`,
corrected the disclosure direction and corrected the administrator wording.
It selected the result without fallback and replayed from 58 cached calls with zero misses.

Typed occurrence-use evidence passed focused controls and name selection,
but its complete repair left the wrong disclosure sentence unchanged.
No participant-correction claim reached its editors.
Different model participation means the typed text is not established as the cause,
but that run does not verify the combined goal.

Generic history descriptions either lost name retention
or suppressed a genuine mentioned-form correction.
The acquisition prototype also returned punctuation and partial-word changes,
so calling every acquired record a naming choice would be false.

Qualified revisions have the stronger positive end-to-end result.
No further wording search or typed-versus-history comparison is required before implementation.
The remaining proof is real acquisition and pre-window production integration.

## Initial archive observations

Candidate discovery initially covers parsed emphasis occurrences.
That is the measured scope,
not a claim to detect every plain-text name.
The span is fixed before a model classifies it;
a model may not expand it or propose a replacement.

The existing block-alignment call carries an auxiliary occurrence-use classification.
It does not add a production generation round.
Classifications describe use,
not translation quality,
officiality,
source equivalence or correctness of entity participation.

- Preserve the primary pairing task and its existing pair-agreement threshold.
- Use the configured preparation roster's existing participation quorum
  as the qualification basis for the new use evidence.
  The observed candidates already have six or seven supporters against a required six of eleven.
- Count each model once per candidate.
- Conflicting,
  malformed,
  missing or insufficiently supported classifications yield no qualified naming evidence.
- Unresolved is not ordinary prose.
- Do not buy additional classification rounds after panel feedback.
- Do not classify generated or revised archive candidates as the initial convention.
- Keep valid primary pairings usable when auxiliary classification is malformed.

One-to-one and empty-side preparation fast paths do not acquire a new model round merely for this feature.
Their lack of observations must be represented honestly.

## Revision qualification

A structured `QualifiedArchiveNamingRevision` records:

- Pinned archive revision and repository-relative path.
- Initial archive identity and exact occurrence anchor.
- Corroborated use kind and its preserved reading/quorum basis.
- Origin and predecessor revisions.
- Exact previous and current changed fragments.
- The current line/fragment scope needed to verify applicability.

Qualification requires:

- A person,
  group or place reference-name kind.
- Exact agreement between archive text,
  offsets,
  quote and hash.
- Same-path pinned ancestry.
- One removed line and one added line whose fragments reconstruct both lines exactly.
- A nonempty two-sided change.
- Changed current span equal to the complete classified occurrence,
  not merely overlap.
- A unique qualifying record.

Withhold evidence,
with a reason,
for work titles,
mentioned forms,
ordinary prose,
unresolved use,
partial spans,
ambiguous revisions,
root or merge origins,
renamed paths,
shallow history and acquisition uncertainty.
Unexpected I/O,
invalid initial anchors and malformed Git output remain errors,
not silent empty evidence.
Malformed auxiliary classifier output instead abstains without invalidating primary pairing.

Every corpus read and history command uses the same intrinsic object semantics:
no replacement refs,
no lazy promisor fetch,
no inherited repository-routing variables,
and `GIT_GRAFT_FILE` forced to the platform null device.
Intrinsic parent headers come from `git cat-file commit`,
not effective `%P` formatting.
This ignores graft overrides per command rather than racing pre/post filesystem checks.

Physical EOF state remains separate from Git's protocol newline.
A qualifying hunk cannot also change line termination.

The initial implementation is conservative.
It does not claim complete history recovery through formatting-only commits,
multi-line edits or renames.
No speculative correspondence is manufactured to improve coverage.

## Meaning of the evidence

The record establishes a local naming choice at one archive occurrence.
It does not establish:

- An official or canonical English name.
- A globally reusable source-to-English equivalence.
- Factual correctness of the surrounding sentence.
- Correctness of which entity acted or participated.
- Authority over supplied contrary identity,
  explicit English-name or work-title evidence.
- Authority to normalize a written or spoken form being discussed.

The original fragment and provenance remain available for audit.
Only the scoped consequence of the qualified naming revision is presented downstream.

## Production integration

Use a closed structured evidence type,
not an arbitrary caller-supplied prose channel.
The renderer must fence corpus-derived text against the complete message it enters.

Evidence reaches the relevant critic,
panel,
editor and selector builders before their final messages are passed to seat-window calculation.
The prototypes injected after that calculation,
so their results do not prove production seating.

- No qualifying evidence must leave existing prompt bytes unchanged.
- The final message digest used for seat selection must include supplied evidence.
- The invoked model IDs must match the window computed from those final messages.
- Cache and twin identity must include normalized structured evidence.
- Artifact generation and readers must preserve the evidence and audit provenance.
- Existing artifacts remain historical results,
  not silently upgraded evidence.
- Re-preparation after archive edits must not promote generated text into an initial-archive convention.

## Verification and completion

Use disposable Git fixtures for qualification and withholding cases.
Exercise the same public interface that production callers use.
Test auxiliary classification independently from primary pairing validity.

After implementation,
automatic acquisition through the compiled build must reproduce correct disclosure direction,
administrator wording and group name together,
without message interception or hand-selected revisions.
Then run the fresh Mio-only pass and perform the full published-page reading.
A compiled integration failure is a production integration or seating problem to diagnose,
not permission to resume broad wording experiments.
