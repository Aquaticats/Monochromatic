# Titles of works stand in italics on the page

Decided by the owner on 2026-09-27 ("Italics for works"),
asked by the whole-package audit.

## What happened

The house rule (`src/house-policy.ts`) said a title stands in quotation marks ("Zero Hour").
Most archives set titles of works in italics
(*Puella Magi Madoka Magica*,
*Needy Streamer Overload*,
*To Live*;
51 of the 92 archives use italics),
and the class one hundred seventy-three pass (`src/corpus-run/archive-italic-title-restore.ts`)
puts the archive's italics back where a slice quoted the same words,
so a judge holding the rule marked the archive's italics down
and a page could carry both styles.

## The rule

- A work's title (a book,
  a game,
  a film,
  a show,
  an album) stands in italics.
- A song,
  a poem,
  an episode or a chapter stands in quotation marks.
- Never the Chinese title marks 《》.

## Options rejected

- Quotation marks everywhere,
    removing the italic restore pass:
    one mark to check,
    but a departure from the archive's style on most pages.
