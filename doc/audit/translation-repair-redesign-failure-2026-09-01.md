actor-attribution,

reviewer,

A normal run with working model and publication infrastructure had to return one complete,
publication-ready document.

## Cross-candidate findings

### Deterministic integrity worked inside its actual boundary

The redesign now has measured guards for structure,
front matter,
links,
media,
contributor identity,
duplicate JSON members,
provider routes,
images,
prompt claims,
terminal node identity,
restart,
cancellation,
and private evidence modes.
GFP repeatedly proved these guards load-bearing.

Those controls prevented damaged or ambiguous artifacts from silently becoming publication authority.
They did not establish semantic fidelity,
idiom,
reference,
actor ownership,
or comparative improvement.
Every candidate that reached complete-page review demonstrated this boundary.

### Producer bookkeeping correlated with fewer admitted authors

Candidate K asked authors to consume readable review units and admitted zero authors.
Candidate L removed that bookkeeping and admitted one author.
Candidate M added six closed attestations and admitted one author.
The candidates also changed rosters,
prompts,
schemas,
and runtime contracts,
so this is an observed correlation and a protocol-burden hypothesis,
not an established cause.
It does not show that values-only authors are sufficient:
Candidates D,
E,
H,
I,
L,
and M all admitted pages that complete reading rejected.

### The third verifier family remained load-bearing and unusable

Strict two-family nonself evidence with three families makes each nonself family necessary for an author from the remaining
family.
Across Candidates I,
L,
and M,
GLM repeatedly ended at a deadline or at 32,000 output tokens before an admissible ballot.
Candidate M's smaller role responses did not change that behavior.

The evidence floor was correctly conservative.
Relaxing it would convert repeated absence of independent evidence into false production eligibility.
That is not authorized.

### Default-route completions reached output limits despite context fit

Complete textual requests fit the live context windows with measured tokenizer evidence.
That did not ensure a forced-tool answer would appear before the output ceiling.
Observed GLM and MiniMax calls reached their output limits with thinking-only or incomplete tool results under provider
defaults.
This proves those exact terminal outcomes,
not a general causal mechanism or run-to-run variability band.
Owner policy forbids non-default reasoning controls,
and spent prompts cannot be resent with different settings.

The unresolved operational question is provider-returned completion allocation under required defaults,
not context-window fit.

### Clean whole-role assertions missed human-visible defects

Candidate M received clean MiniMax challenges in both roles,
yet complete reading found defects in both fidelity and publication language.
The result does not prove MiniMax generally cannot review.
It does prove that one clean role assertion is not sufficient evidence for this page.

Candidate H's complete matrices produced no admitted ballots,
while Candidate M's compact clean assertions admitted evidence that missed defects.
The redesign moved between unreturnable exhaustive evidence and returnable but under-discriminating evidence without finding
an interface that satisfied both constraints.

### Immutable slots preserved syntax but could still lose document structure

Candidate M preserved model-external Markdown syntax but collapsed a source and archive paragraph boundary inside a mutable
slot.
Earlier candidates also showed that immutable boundaries could create awkward inline spacing or constrain rhetorical
relations.
The shell reduced structural authority;
it did not make every source paragraph,
discourse boundary,
or cross-slot relation immutable.

### More static candidates did not create a publication-ready survivor

Candidates D through M tried fixed priority,
serial editing,
comparative selection,
closed-world ballots,
candidate-scoped ballots,
readable review units,
lean authors,
and risk-attested authors.
Whenever a page admitted,
complete reading found publication-blocking defects.
Adding another static seat would repeat the measured pattern unless its contract and evidence mechanism changed materially.
The stop condition forbids testing that speculation as Candidate N.

## Failure hypotheses for future research

These are hypotheses,
not authorization to implement.
A future team should test them only after the owner explicitly reopens architecture work.

### Hypothesis 1: the quality contract exceeds one-pass model reliability

One whole-page author must simultaneously preserve detailed events,
actors,
chronology,
voice,
idiom,
structure,
media relation,
and contributor authority.
Across distinct protocols,
models returned complete-looking pages with sparse but publication-blocking defects.
A static finite graph without correction may not reach the required reliability with the screened roster.

### Hypothesis 2: available family diversity is nominal rather than operational

The design needs two independent nonself families,
but the available vision-capable routes did not all return admissible complete-page evidence under default provider behavior.
Qwen often completed but retained page defects.
MiniMax alternated between usable concise review and author truncation.
GLM repeatedly spent its output budget before tool completion.
The roster therefore lacked operationally independent evidence even when three family names were manifested.

### Hypothesis 3: exhaustive evidence and bounded output are in tension

Closed-world matrices made omissions visible but were difficult for routes to return.
First-defect challenges fit better but made clean verdicts too opaque.
A future interface may need runtime-derived evidence smaller than exhaustive model-authored matrices without treating silence
as cleanliness.
No tested candidate found that boundary.

### Hypothesis 4: model-independent source analysis is not yet semantic enough

The runtime can derive slots,
spans,
relations,
and integrity digests,
but it cannot prove that an English anchor realizes a Chinese proposition or preserves its actor.
Model-authored realization maps were untrusted,
and verifier checks were either unusable or fallible.
A future direction may require stronger source analysis outside the generation model,
but current deterministic parsing does not provide it.

### Hypothesis 5: the no-correction constraint and normal-return guarantee may be jointly unsatisfied by this roster

The user correctly rejected suspension,
continuation,
retry,
and serial quality layering because they leave normal completion unbounded or non-monotonic.
The static alternatives did terminate,
but termination repeatedly produced no eligible page.
The evidence may indicate a real feasibility conflict for this provider roster and task contract,
not merely a missing prompt formulation.
