One keyboard map, with editing and controls owning their keys

Purpose: complete D25's whole-map IntelliJ alignment pass without asking about individual key labels. D25's Ctrl+F and Ctrl+O reservations are settled, and the human accepted the whole map as built (D102). The consequential concern is preventing caret motion or a focused button from also changing playback.

This is an HTML behavior sandbox, not an Android/Slint screen mockup. It reads no files, plays no audio and implements no matcher. Platform shortcut delivery, media keys, IME behavior and native accessibility are unverified. The map and the playback-area scope are accepted; this page does not prove any platform delivers the keys.

Accepted map

    Search and Settings use current IntelliJ precedents. Reveal-current uses its go-to-location precedent. Transport and mode keys are product exceptions, not claims that IntelliJ performs music operations. The macOS track fallback uses the IDE's previous/next-tab chords rather than Control+arrow workspace chords.

    Advantages: one consistent action vocabulary, no in-app volume shortcuts, preserved caret and widget keys. Tradeoffs: some familiar draft assignments change, and OS reservations still need native verification. There is no valid reason to offer the superseded volume draft as another option.

    Inspect ownership and consequences

    Focus the playback shortcut area itself to try its transport keys; its buttons and slider retain their own keys. Focus the query or seek slider to see those controls retain their own keys. Space on a focused button activates that button only once. Application shortcuts are active within this demonstration and its popup, not the surrounding review notes.
