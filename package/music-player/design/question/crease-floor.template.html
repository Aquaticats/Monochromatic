P7.5 · 7.5mm total minimum (Selected)

Pros

  • Shows the actual player with Search closed, full deck and track list; its current-row surface extends into the center while the sampled labeled nodes stay outside the estimated crease.
  • Names a 7.5mm total information floor even if a future device has a narrower crease; ordinary component spacing in this Fold capture can exceed that minimum.

Cons

  • Provides less nominal total separation than P14/P20 on other hardware where the crease is narrow.
  • This one Fold capture does not certify glyph ink, long track names, other scales or keyboard-open fit.

My read: Recommend confirming your 7.5mm floor if these added player views are what you meant. Its total threshold is independent of decorative backgrounds, borders and hit regions crossing the center.

P14 · 14mm total minimum

Pros

  • Moves right-side player headings, track text and Search information farther from left-side information while preserving the full-width left browser and deck.
  • Does not move the highlighted row background, divider or hit regions to meet an informational floor.

Cons

  • Uses more of the right-side text region than P7.5; wider content and focused-keyboard states remain untested.
  • No improvement in comprehension or touch interaction has been demonstrated by that added space.

My read: Second choice if you want a wider physical baseline after inspecting both player and Search, not a mandate to narrow the folder browser.

P20 · 20mm total minimum

Pros

  • Creates the greatest sampled separation of right-side text from the left deck and browser, without introducing a central structural stripe.
  • Retains the full-size left deck and its browser instead of compressing their mode targets.

Cons

  • The empty Search instruction wraps earlier in the native image; long track and result titles could lose visible space.
  • The additional clearance has no established readability benefit and other text scales remain untested.

My read: Last choice unless the stronger independent information floor is specifically desired after seeing the player as well as Search.
