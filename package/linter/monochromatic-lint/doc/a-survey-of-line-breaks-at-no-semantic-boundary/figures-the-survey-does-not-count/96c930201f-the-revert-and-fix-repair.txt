style(git-policy-cli): join the doc lines the old hard wrap left alone

Issue 6642. Merge `beb61ec46b` ran the then-current
`markdown/semantic-line-breaks` fix over 471 Rust files of this package, and
that fix broke a line after a comma or a clause without joining what the old
hard wrap had cut, so a word that ended an old line stands alone at no
boundary and the rule reports nothing there.

The repair is the fixed tool's own output, not a hand edit: for each of the
471 files the merge changed, the pre-sweep copy (`beb61ec46b^1:<path>`) went
through the installed `monochromatic-lint` `0.5.2-alpha.24` with a
configuration naming `markdown/semantic-line-breaks` alone, and that output
replaces a doc paragraph only where the paragraph's words are the same in the
pre-sweep file and on this branch, compared with every comment marker and
every whitespace character removed.

771 doc paragraphs were repaired in 367 files, netting 90 fewer doc lines.
Every changed line is a `//!` or `///` line, and the text of every changed
file with its doc markers and every whitespace character removed is identical
before and after, which a probe that changed one word shows the comparison can
see.

Six paragraphs stay as the branch held them, because another round reworded or
added them since the sweep; none of the six holds a one- or two-word line that
ends at no break point.

The measurement and its scripts are the run's own; the round is unapproved and
non-final.
