# Design commitments

Part of [the family README](../README.md).

The commitments are the design rules this package keeps,
each with the owner's ruling or the measurement behind it,
filed by subject in the files listed here;
a new commitment goes into the file whose subject it belongs to.

- [Design commitments: evidence and structure](design-commitments-evidence-and-structure.md):
  what counts as evidence,
  how structure is detected per document,
  how refusals are handled,
  and how prompt text is fenced.
- [Design commitments: sheets, probes and audits](design-commitments-sheets-probes-and-audits.md):
  the grading sheets,
  the binding of a grade to its draw,
  the introduced-defect probe,
  and the audit of the last stage.
- [Design commitments: providers and seats](design-commitments-providers-and-seats.md):
  judge seats that follow the provider serving them,
  re-asked replies,
  the NeuralWatt reasoning parameter,
  and picture readers joining a route.
- [Design commitments: shipping and the archive](design-commitments-shipping-and-the-archive.md):
  stub markers in the archive,
  whitespace Markdown renders alike,
  a run that always ships,
  a settled page shipped with its defects reported,
  and a slice keeping the archive's wording.
- [Design commitments: votes, repairs and run-offs](design-commitments-votes-repairs-and-run-offs.md):
  confirmation quorums,
  panel verdicts,
  edits shed from a winning patch,
  rechecked rewrites,
  markup atoms,
  neutral claims,
  the standing directive on quality,
  the run-offs,
  the polish gate's tie,
  and two answering judges deciding with no weight floor.
- [Design commitments: the rulings of 2026-10-21](design-commitments-rulings-of-2026-10-21.md):
  the full record of the polish gate's tie rule
  and of every ballot giving its reason before its choice.
- [Design commitments: the rulings of 2026-10-22](design-commitments-rulings-of-2026-10-22.md):
  two rulings of the lead,
  open to the owner's veto:
  the entry in flight when a run stops stays resumable,
  and only dryness of every provider ends the run.
- [Design commitments: the consistency pass](design-commitments-consistency-pass.md):
  repeated source lines rendered one way,
  and the pass that carries words and leaves every mark where it was.
- [Design commitments: tense and dictionary entries](design-commitments-tense-and-dictionary-entries.md):
  the past-tense rule and its deterministic aid,
  and the dictionary entry for 扑面而来.
