# Design commitments: rulings of 2026-10-21

Two owner rulings of 2026-10-21 that answer the questions the rotation 21 read left open.
Part of [Design commitments](design-commitments.md),
which lists each beside the earlier ruling it extends.

## Every ballot and verdict gives its reason before its choice

Owner,
2026-10-21,
answering the side lead of the rotation 21 read,
whether the contest,
polish and select ballots should give their reason first as the issue panel does:
"Reason first everywhere".
This extends the 2026-09-27 ruling ("Reason before vote") from the panel verdict to the other ballots and verdicts.
The schema declares and requires the reason first,
and the sheet's reply instruction names it first,
in the contest ballot (which the lane contest,
the consolidation gate and the polish gate share),
the select ballot,
the absolute naturalness review,
the coverage judge
and the archive block review (whose `finding` is its reason).
The introduced-defect wire stays as it was,
because its reason is empty on a verdict that finds no defect;
the rewrite wire's explanation follows the translation it explains;
the rendering audit gives its verdict and then a reason for each finding;
and the attestation wires carry no reason.
A reason first can move votes,
as the 2026-09-27 ruling noted,
and no bench measures it;
a reply cut short now loses its choice instead of its reason.
The cache versions of every sheet changed moved with it (ledger B732).

## A polish gate that ties ships the polished text

Owner,
2026-10-21,
answering the class C question of the rotation 21 read,
where the gate of CuspariaKLSY21 slice 1 split base,
base,
polished,
polished and shipped the base,
whether a polish gate tie should be run off:
"Polished text wins ties".
Base,
base,
polished,
polished and base,
polished both ship the polished text;
below the minimum ballot count nothing changes,
and the gate logs the tie and the rule.
A tie needs a ballot on each side,
so a refusal beside one vote is not a tie.
The run-off of a tied consolidation slate (2026-10-11,
"Run off the tied finalists") is unchanged:
there the standing ships on a second tie (ledger B730).
